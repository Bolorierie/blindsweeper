BlindSweeper

Minesweeper as a run. Every safe cell you dig scores chips × mult. Each round has a target. Hit it and you cash out to a shop where relics bend the rules in your favour.

You have three hearts for the whole run. A mine costs one. Every third round is a boss with a twist. Targets outgrow honest play around round seven, so build something.

Dig: click or tap. Flag: right-click, long-press, or toggle flag mode. First dig is always safe.
